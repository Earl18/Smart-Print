How it works

Four steps, about two minutes from send to print.

  1. Choose your paper size, color mode and copies on the kiosk.
  2. Send your file — scan the QR code, use Bluetooth, or plug in a USB drive.
  3. Review the price and pay with cash or e-wallet.
  4. Collect your prints from the tray. Your file is deleted after printing.
